THE STORY BEHIND THE MUSIC

Meet Vansh Antil

A young artist from a small village, carrying a big dream through music.

01

Where It All Started

Palri, Sonipat

Vansh Antil comes from Palri, a small village near Sonipat. Away from the big music studios, his journey started with nothing more than a dream and a love for music.

Music Since Childhood

Vansh started writing songs when he was still a child. What started as a simple passion slowly became something he wanted to turn into his future.

02 — THE PEOPLE BEHIND THE DREAM

His Family Believed First.

Vansh shared his dream with his parents when he was in 10th class. Instead of stopping him, they chose to support him.

His father Bhagat Singh and his mother Suman Tushir stood beside him and encouraged him to follow his passion.

THE FIRST STEP

Way To Hell

With no professional equipment and no big studio behind him, Vansh uploaded his first song "Way To Hell" on YouTube.

It wasn't about having the perfect setup. It was about finally taking the first step.
